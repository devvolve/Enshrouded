# The Triad’s Feast  
A Psychological Horror Narrative Adventure  
Developed in Unreal Engine 5  


## Overview
**The Triad’s Feast** is a third-person psychological horror narrative adventure that blends survival mechanics, puzzle-solving, and dynamic moral decision-making.  
Inspired by **Silent Hill**, **Spec Ops: The Line**, **The Evil Within**, and **Hellblade: Senua’s Sacrifice**, the game focuses on psychological tension, ethical ambiguity, and atmosphere-driven storytelling.

Created for Windows PC and WebGL, the game places players in an abandoned ritual town ruled by **The Triad**, a being born from chaos who feeds on suffering. When the protagonist’s family is abducted, their rescue becomes a twisted game of survival, morality, and mind-bending trials.

## Setup Instructions
1. Clone the repository.
2. Open the project using **Unreal Engine 5.x**.
3. Ensure the following plugins are enabled:
   - Enhanced Input
   - Blueprint Debugging Tools (optional)
4. Open the main level: /Content/Levels/MainLevel.umap

5. Press **Play** to begin.

## Features

### 🎮 Core Gameplay
- First-Person exploration through atmospheric light/dark zones  
- Psychological and environmental puzzles  
- Survival encounters requiring stealth, resource management, and decision-making  
- Dynamic story responses based on player actions  

### 👁️ Light & Darkness System
- Darkness spawns shadow entities  
- Light protects but must be managed strategically  
- Flashlight reveals hidden paths, clues, and threats  
- being detected by the Shadow monsters will drain sanity

### 🧠 Sanity System (Enshroud Meter)
- Looking at entities or staying in dark areas reduces sanity  
- Low sanity triggers:
- screen distortion  
- whispers and hallucinations  
- hunt phases  

### 🔦 Flashlight & Power Management
- Flashlight consumes battery  
- Batteries and resources are limited  
- Essential for puzzles and survival  

### 🧩 Puzzles
- Light-based and physics-based puzzles  
- Key item interactions through the Inventory system  

### 👣 Stealth / Hide & Seek
- Hide in:
- lockers  
- under desks  
- behind furniture  
- Avoid enemies during hunt phases  

### 🎒 Inventory & Interaction
- Collect notes, keys, and tools  
- Press **E** to interact with objects  
- Inventory stores collected items for puzzle progression

## Controls
- **WASD** – Move  
- **Mouse** – Look  
- **E** – Interact / Pick Up  
- **Shift** – Sprint  
- **F** – Flashlight Toggle  
- **Space** – Vault / Context Actions (if applicable)
